# Sources — Wednesday 18 November 2026

Every story in `entry.png` was reported on 18 November 2026 (events that
happened, or were first reported, that day in any time zone). Cross-checked
across several briefings; the wording in the image stays close to what was
reported, with no speculation presented as fact.

1. **Xi meets Putin in Shenzhen (APEC).** Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin began
   talks in Shenzhen on Wednesday, one day before a first-ever trilateral
   summit with President Donald Trump. (AFP / Associated Press / BBC)
2. **U.S. strikes Qeshm Island; Iran answers in the Gulf.** U.S. fighter
   jets struck a Revolutionary Guards missile launch site on Qeshm Island;
   Iran said it targeted Kuwait's Ali Al Salem Air Base (hosting U.S. forces)
   and Bahrain said it intercepted attacks before dawn. (Reuters / Wall
   Street Journal)
3. **Ukraine hits the Crimea (Kerch) bridge.** Ukrainian drones and missiles
   partially destroyed the bridge; part of its span fell into the strait,
   the second day of strikes. (Multiple outlets)
4. **Peltola wins Alaska.** Democrat Mary Peltola defeated Republican
   incumbent Dan Sullivan, giving Democrats a 56–44 Senate majority.
   (Associated Press)
5. **U.S. loses measles-elimination status.** Held since 2000; the Pan
   American Health Organization concluded the virus spread continuously for
   over a year. 5,412 confirmed cases in 2026 as of 12 November, 10 deaths.
   (PAHO / CDC)
6. **Ebola in DR Congo passes 11,000 cases.** More than doubled in three
   months; the second-largest Ebola outbreak on record, with children
   bearing the heaviest toll. (UN / WHO reporting)
7. **Ticker.** Voyager 1 reached one light-day from Earth on 18 November
   2026 at 5:16:07 a.m. ET, about 16 billion miles (NASA JPL). Nvidia
